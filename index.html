Grammar Basics

Simple explanations with examples.

Present Simple

Use it for habits, routines and facts.

Example: I study English every day.

Past Simple

Use it for completed actions in the past.

Example: I studied English yesterday.

Future Simple

Use it for actions that will happen later.

Example: I will study English tomorrow.

Present Continuous

Use it for an action happening now.

Example: I am studying English now.
